Common questions

Before you reach out.

A few things clients usually ask before starting a project.

What kind of Shopify projects do you work on?

I work on everything from small fixes and custom sections to complete Shopify stores, redesigns, 1:1 store builds, and custom functionality.

I only need a small fix. Can you help?

Yes. You don't need a full project to work with me. Send me the issue, store link, or screenshots, and I'll review what needs to be done and let you know the cost and timeline.

How long does a Shopify project take?

It depends on the scope. Small fixes are usually completed within 24 hours, a simple store takes 2–3 days, and a fully branded, custom store takes 5–7 days. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with existing Shopify stores?

Yes. I regularly work on existing stores, whether you need a few changes, a redesign, new sections, custom functionality, or improvements to the overall store.

How do revisions work?

Revisions are included within the agreed project scope. If you decide to add new sections, features, or requirements outside the original scope, I'll let you know about the additional cost before starting.

How do we communicate during the project?

Mainly through WhatsApp — it keeps things quick and simple to share screenshots, links, or updates as we go.

How do we get started?

Send me your store link, reference website, design, or simply explain what you need. I'll review the project, confirm the scope, timeline, and price, and we can get started.
